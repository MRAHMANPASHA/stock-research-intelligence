ABOUT

About this tool

Stock Research Intelligence turns a single topic into a complete stock-content research pack. Type what you want to create for — a holiday, a season, a profession, a hobby — and the tool studies what buyers are actually searching for right now, then hands you a brief you can act on the same day.

Every run produces a research brief with the timing windows that matter (when buyers search, when to upload, when to post), the audiences who buy, the activities and scenes they want to see, and notes for each major market. Under the brief sits a ranked list of opportunities — concrete photo, vector and video concepts, each scored for demand against competition, each with its own reference picture.

And because a concept is only half the work, every opportunity arrives with nine ready-to-use prompts — three for design, three for images, three for video — plus one-click CSV exports that drop straight into Google Sheets. A built-in Awareness Days calendar keeps the whole year's events in front of you, so the next brief is always planned before the season starts.

How to use the prompts — two options

Option A — Copy a prompt and create it yourself

Go to the Prompts tab, press Copy on any Design / Image / Video prompt, and paste that prompt into your own image/video generator or tool to create the content yourself. You get the prompt exactly as it was in the sheet.

Option B — Leave it as is / export it

If you don't want to create it yourself, leave the data as is — or download a full tab as CSV and import it back into Google Sheets. Nothing changes; the data stays in sheet format.

Sheet structure: Briefs = 1 row per run · Opportunities = one row per opportunity · Prompts = 9 rows per opportunity · Keys are the same everywhere: run_id → opp_id → prompt_key, so VLOOKUP / import back into the sheet stays easy.

Stock buyers brief 4–6 weeks before each date, so the badges show exactly where you are in the prep window.
